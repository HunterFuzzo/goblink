FAQ

Is Blink available for Windows?

Yes! Blink is officially available for Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit). You can download the Windows installer (.exe) directly from the link above. It includes Caps Lock LED, Floating HUD, and iPhone Vibration modes.

I subscribed. How do I start using it?

After completing your subscription on Stripe, simply open Blink on your Mac or Windows PC. Your subscription is automatically detected and bound to your device hardware (HWID) within seconds. You can immediately press ⌃⌥⌘ (or Ctrl + Alt + Space on Windows) to start analyzing questions.

How accurate are the answers?

Blink uses state-of-the-art vision reasoning models with dedicated academic benchmarks, delivering over 98% accuracy on multiple-choice, STEM, law, and medical quizzes in less than a second.

Does it work with short-answer questions?

Yes. While multiple-choice questions can be conveyed via tactile vibrations or Caps Lock pulses, detailed short answers can be viewed in the discreet HUD mode or directly in your paired Blink companion.

How do the stealth modes work (LED & iPhone Vibration)?

Blink operates completely behind the scenes. In Caps Lock mode, your keyboard indicator flashes silently (e.g. 2 pulses for answer B). In iPhone Vibration mode, your phone buzzes subtly in your pocket via local Wi-Fi without turning on its display.

Can exam proctoring software detect Blink?

Blink is an independent native background process. It does not hook into web browsers, does not inject into web pages, does not alter clipboard content, and opens no intrusive windows during hotkey captures.

Which operating systems and hardware are supported?

Blink supports Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit), as well as macOS (Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4 and Intel Macs running macOS 12 Monterey up to macOS 15 Sequoia).
